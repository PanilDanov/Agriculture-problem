# AI and Super Intelligence disclosure

Public project name: **orbitFarm**. AgriWise/AgroWise names in older discussions,
data filenames and Python package identifiers are historical technical names.

This disclosure covers recorded AI assistance in this repository and the current
integration work. It distinguishes generative assistance from trained prediction
models and from observations retrieved from external data providers. Its
machine-readable companion is [docs/ai-usage.json](docs/ai-usage.json).

## Trained models and real data

The application also uses statistical and machine-learning estimators fitted to
cited agricultural data. These prediction models are a separate use of AI from
generative coding or image generation:

- Ten national annual-history models use FAOSTAT yield histories.
- Twenty experimental provincial pre-season/mid-season models use actual MAPA
  statistics and NASA POWER monthly weather at representative province points.
- Eight original wheat/maize regional models use compatible registered inputs,
  including AgERA5 weather and crop-mask support where available.

Training procedures, holdout errors, baselines and limitations are described in
[the model documentation](yield_prediction/docs/inference_service.md). Model
estimates, reference-screen scores and calculated water quantities are derived
outputs. They are not measurements of the selected field. The provincial models
are experimental; their calendar dates are representative monthly scenarios.

NASA POWER observations, MAPA/FAOSTAT statistics and other credited source data
are retrieved data, not invented generative-AI observations. Normalization,
feature calculation and model inference are processing steps. Synthetic test
fixtures and the explicitly labelled demo scenarios are distinct from those
source records. Source URLs, observation cutoffs, retrieval dates and hashes are
retained where provided by the data pipeline.

## Artwork labelling and provenance

The website places a visible **AI-generated artwork** mark on the farm composite,
the scanner illustration, the standalone satellite and crop-illustration groups.
The farm composite includes generated scenery, crops and the dog. The mark
identifies artwork; it does not label NASA observations as generated images.
It remains visible during reduced-motion rendering and does not intercept plot
selection or other controls.

Historical prompts, source masters and raster assets are preserved unchanged.
The visible mark is rendered in HTML/CSS over or within the illustration group.
Screenshots or demo recordings of these views should retain that mark. When
presenting a standalone source image separately, include a visible generation
mark in that presentation as well; the unmodified source PNG is not itself a
watermarked export.

The sprites are generated pixel-art-style rasters. They are illustrative, not
biological growth models or NASA mission imagery. Orbit-1 is a fictional project
character with an orbitFarm emblem. The project is independent of NASA and is
not presented as an official NASA product or endorsement.
