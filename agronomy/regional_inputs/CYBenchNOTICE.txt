Adaptation by orbitFarm contributors, 15 November 2026
----------------------------------------------------
The two JSON files are filtered, reformatted extracts of the original Spain
maize/wheat crop-calendar, location, weather and soil CSV tables for ES112
(Lugo). Dates were converted to ISO strings, numeric values serialized to JSON,
and recurring calendar days converted to season windows for the 2026 scenario.
The files add observation-availability and scenario metadata. Historical daily
weather ends in 2023; the recurring calendar is not observed 2026 sowing data.
Source table hashes and the extraction procedure are retained in
source_provenance.json and yield_prediction/prepare_published_region.py.

The independently sourced IGN selection geometry in registry.json is separate
from CY-Bench's historical crop-mask aggregation geometry; its attribution and
CC BY 4.0 conditions remain recorded in source_provenance.json. This notice
does not relicense IGN geometry, original orbitFarm code, or all model weights
under EUPL, and does not claim that source datasets are Apache-2.0 material.
